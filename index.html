What Was This?

Regions of War was more than just a mere game, it was an entire world built on the collective minds of creative, optimistic, and power-hungry teenagers eager to leave their mark on the fictional planet of Gaeia. There was no script, no set timeline. Just diplomacy, rivalry, alliances, and the stories they made together.

This site exists to preserve that history: the nations, the wars, the lore, and the moments that made it unforgettable.

Who Was This?

The first iteration of Regions of War all started with a guy named Ivan, taking inspiration inspiration from a popular global online game. At first, there was only Kent, Rey, Ken, and Cian alongside Ivan. As the first version of RoW blew up in popularity and eventually ended, the playerbase wanted more. Hence RoW: Rekindled Flame was created, bringing in players previously usually offline to contribute more greatly such as Sirius, Serrano, and Rayce.

Built with love, for the nations and the friends who ran them.
